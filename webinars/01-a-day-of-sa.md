## Description

The role of a Solutions Architect is often misunderstood as purely technical or certification-driven. This session cuts through theory to show the practical reality of the role.

## Topics Covered

- Defining the Solutions Architect from a practitioner perspective
- Core responsibilities: bridging business requirements and cloud architecture
- Architecture design patterns and trade-off analysis
- Production concerns: cost optimization, security, and operational readiness
- Advanced systems: multi-region design, migration strategy, and compliance
- Essential skills for modern Solutions Architects
- Q&A session

## Key Takeaways

1. Define the SA's Role - understanding core responsibilities at the intersection of technical design and client advisory
2. Collaborate with Others - how SAs work within cross-functional teams (engineering, security, sales, leadership)
3. Frameworks - applying Well-Architected Framework, trade-off analysis, and architecture governance


## Q&A During the Webinar

### How can a DevOps engineer transition to become a solutions architect?

For DevOps engineers, solutions architecture is primarily a design and advisory role, not an implementation role. Most "architecture" work is about understanding business requirements, evaluating trade-offs between approaches, and communicating recommendations. DevOps engineers already know infrastructure, automation, and cloud operations. The perspective might differ - operations vs design - but the technical foundation is very similar.

The specific skills to learn: how to run architecture workshops, how to present designs to non-technical stakeholders, how to evaluate build vs buy decisions. After 2-3 months of learning SA-specific design patterns and communication skills, a DevOps engineer should be ready to transition.

For cloud architecture specifically, you need system design thinking, cost modeling, and security architecture depth. This is what DevOps engineers have been working adjacent to their entire career.

### How to narrow down what to focus on when preparing for SA interviews?

The most important parts to focus on:

1. Learn how to design cloud architectures with clear trade-offs
2. Understand the Well-Architected Framework - it is the common language
3. Practice whiteboard architecture design - communication while diagramming is the top skill
4. Cloud services breadth - know the 50 most common services across compute, storage, networking, security
5. Cost optimization - every architecture answer should include cost reasoning
6. Security - proactively address IAM, encryption, and network security in every design

For projects: pick a specific workload type (e-commerce, data platform, migration). Design the architecture, estimate the cost, document the trade-offs, and build a proof of concept in Terraform. This gives you interview material and demonstrates hands-on credibility.
