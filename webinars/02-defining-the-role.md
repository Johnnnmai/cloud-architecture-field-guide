## Description

What do companies actually hire Solutions Architects for? This session breaks down the role using real job market data and hiring manager perspectives.

## Topics Covered

- SA role types: Customer-Facing, Internal, and Specialist
- Skills analysis from 860+ job descriptions
- How SA responsibilities vary by company size and industry
- The gap between job postings and reality
- Career progression paths (IC vs management)
- Certifications: which ones matter and when
- Q&A session

## Key Takeaways

1. Three Role Types - Customer-Facing SA (52%), Internal SA (39%), Specialist SA (9%) require different skill mixes
2. Skills Beyond Cloud - 87% of roles require communication, cost optimization, or security skills beyond cloud platform expertise
3. Hands-On Matters - 72% of roles expect implementation ability, not just design

## Q&A During the Webinar

### What certifications should I get first?

Start with the platform your target companies use most. AWS Solutions Architect Associate is the most requested certification overall. If you are targeting Microsoft-ecosystem companies, Azure Solutions Architect Expert is the right choice.

Certifications are trust signals, not skill demonstrations. They get your resume past the filter. Real architecture experience, portfolio projects, and interview performance determine the outcome. A candidate with one certification and a well-documented architecture case study outperforms a candidate with five certifications and no portfolio.

### Is multi-cloud experience required?

For most roles, deep expertise in one platform is more valuable than shallow knowledge of all three. However, interviewers expect you to reason about multi-cloud trade-offs. Knowing that DynamoDB's pricing model differs from Cosmos DB's, or that Azure's networking model differs from AWS's, demonstrates architecture thinking beyond vendor lock-in.

The recommendation: go deep on one platform (certifications, hands-on projects, production experience), then learn the key differentiators of the other two platforms at a conceptual level.

### How important is coding ability for SAs?

Coding is not the primary skill, but hands-on technical ability is essential. Most SA roles expect you to write IaC (Terraform/CloudFormation), scripting (Python/Bash), and read application code. You do not need to be a full-stack developer, but you need to be comfortable building proof of concepts and debugging configurations.

In interviews, "I can design it but I cannot build it" is a red flag. "I designed this architecture and here is the Terraform module that deploys it" is the standard.
