## Description

Real hiring trends and live architecture challenges. This session covers what SA interviews actually look like, based on 65+ interview experiences across cloud providers, consulting firms, and enterprise companies.

## Topics Covered

- Interview process breakdown by company type (cloud provider, consulting, enterprise)
- Architecture design round deep dive - how to approach whiteboard challenges
- Common question patterns across 48 companies
- Live architecture challenge walkthrough
- What separates candidates who get offers from those who do not
- Salary negotiation for SA roles
- Q&A session

## Key Takeaways

1. Architecture Design is the Decisive Round - this is where interviews are won or lost; practice communicating while designing
2. Clarify Before Designing - always start with requirements gathering, not solutions
3. Cost and Security are Mandatory - every architecture answer must address both, even if not explicitly asked
4. War Stories Win - real migration failures and lessons learned are more compelling than textbook answers
