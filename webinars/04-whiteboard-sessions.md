## Description

Analyzing real architecture design challenges from SA interviews. This session walks through complete whiteboard sessions, demonstrating how to approach architecture problems from requirements to diagram to trade-off analysis.

## Topics Covered

- Whiteboard challenge methodology - the 4-step approach
- Live walkthrough: designing a multi-region e-commerce platform
- Live walkthrough: migrating a legacy banking application to AWS
- Common mistakes and how to avoid them
- How interviewers evaluate architecture design sessions
- Practice strategies for improving whiteboard performance
- Q&A session

## Key Takeaways

1. The 4-Step Method - Clarify, Design, Deep Dive, Trade-offs. Every whiteboard challenge should follow this structure
2. Communication is the Product - the interviewer cannot see your thinking unless you narrate while drawing
3. Trade-offs are the Signal - proactively stating what you gave up in your design demonstrates architecture maturity
4. Practice Out Loud - the most effective preparation is designing architectures while explaining your reasoning to someone else
