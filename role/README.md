## Key Takeaways

### What is a "Solutions Architect" in 2026?

- It is a role that has evolved significantly. Solutions architects own the intersection of business requirements, technical constraints, and cloud platform capabilities. But titles are inconsistent - "Solutions Architect" means different things at different companies.
- Three types of roles hide under the same title:
  - Customer-Facing SA (52.1%) - works directly with clients to design cloud architectures, leads presales engagements, and drives adoption
  - Internal SA (38.7%) - designs systems for internal engineering teams, sets architectural standards, reviews designs across teams
  - Specialist SA (9.2%) - deep expertise in one domain (security, data, networking, AI/ML) applied across customer or internal engagements

- It is fundamentally a cross-functional role. 87.3% of roles need skills beyond cloud platform knowledge. Only 5.8% are pure design and advisory. You need hands-on implementation ability, cost optimization skills, client communication, and often security expertise.

### What they actually build

- Architecture design and migration dominate. Cloud architecture design and migration planning appear in 62.4% of all jobs, cost optimization in 31.7%. Together they cover 70%+ of architecture scenarios. If you learn these two disciplines deeply, you cover most of the work.
- The number one problem SAs solve is translating business requirements into scalable cloud architectures (41.3% of architecture scenarios). Not glamorous - it is reducing complexity and making the right trade-offs at scale.
- Security and compliance are universal. Every domain (healthcare, finance, government, enterprise) has the same challenge: maintaining security posture while enabling speed and innovation.

### Skills that matter

- AWS is the most requested platform (68.4%). After that: Azure (51.2%), GCP (28.7%), Terraform/IaC (45.3%), Kubernetes (38.1%), networking (35.6%), security architecture (33.2%).
- Pure coding skills are secondary for most SA roles. Only 8.3% of roles focus on software development as a primary responsibility. 71.8% expect you to read code and write scripts, not build applications. Focus on architecture patterns and cloud services first.
- 87.3% require cross-functional skills beyond technical depth - client communication, presales, cost optimization, project scoping.

### What actually gets you hired

- Architecture trade-off thinking is the differentiator. 54.7% of SA roles explicitly require the ability to evaluate multiple design options and justify decisions with clear trade-off analysis.
- Hands-on implementation wins over whiteboard-only design. 63.2% of SA roles require some level of hands-on work (proof of concepts, reference architectures, IaC templates).
- 94.2% of roles are applied/delivery, not pure advisory. The market wants people who design and build, not people who draw diagrams and hand them off.
