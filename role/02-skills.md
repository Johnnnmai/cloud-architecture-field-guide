# Solutions Architect Skills Analysis

Generated from 860+ job descriptions extracted from major job boards.

I searched for jobs containing "Solutions Architect" keyword from major US tech hubs, London, and remote positions over a 4-week period. I did it at the beginning of February 2026, so it mostly contains jobs published in January 2026.

Summary

- 52% of roles are customer-facing (presales, consulting, professional services)
- 87% need skills beyond cloud platform expertise - it is a cross-functional role
- 68.4% of roles mention AWS - the most common platform across all jobs
- 87.3% of SA roles require client communication and stakeholder management
- Enterprise (412 jobs) > Cloud-Native Startups (248 jobs) > Consulting/SI (200 jobs)


## "Solutions Architect" Job Types

Job positions I analyzed fall into these categories:

- Customer-Facing SA
- Internal SA
- Specialist SA

### Customer-Facing SA: 448 jobs (52.1%)

Working directly with clients to design and sell cloud solutions.

What they own:

- Technical presales and solution design
- Architecture workshops and discovery sessions
- Reference architecture creation and proof of concepts
- Customer success and adoption driving
- Technical relationship management with key accounts

Example responsibilities:

- "Design cloud architectures aligned with customer business objectives"
- "Lead technical workshops to assess customer readiness for cloud migration"
- "Build proof of concepts demonstrating solution viability"
- "Present architecture proposals to C-level stakeholders"
- "Drive adoption of cloud services across customer organizations"

Position title examples

- Solutions Architect
- Senior Solutions Architect
- Principal Solutions Architect
- Cloud Solutions Architect
- Enterprise Solutions Architect
- Customer Solutions Architect


### Internal SA: 333 jobs (38.7%)

Designing systems and setting standards for internal engineering teams.

What they own:

- Internal architecture standards and governance
- System design reviews and approval processes
- Cross-team integration and dependency management
- Technology strategy and platform selection
- Architecture decision records and documentation

Example responsibilities:

- "Define architecture standards and best practices across engineering teams"
- "Review and approve system designs for new product features"
- "Lead architecture governance board and design review process"
- "Evaluate build vs buy decisions for infrastructure components"
- "Create architecture decision records for critical system changes"

Position title examples

- Solutions Architect - Internal
- Enterprise Architect
- Staff Solutions Architect
- Technical Architect
- Architecture Lead


### Specialist SA: 79 jobs (9.2%)

Deep expertise in one domain applied across engagements.

What they own:

- Domain-specific architecture patterns (security, data, networking, AI/ML)
- Best practices and reference architectures for their specialty
- Technical mentorship in their domain
- Cross-cutting concerns that span multiple teams or customers

Position title examples

- Security Solutions Architect
- Data Solutions Architect
- Network Architect
- AI/ML Solutions Architect
- Integration Architect


### How to Tell the Difference

The key question is: Does this role face customers, or internal teams?

Customer-Facing SA:

- Leads presales engagements
- Runs architecture workshops with clients
- Builds proof of concepts for sales cycles
- Presents to C-level stakeholders
- Drives cloud adoption and expansion

Internal SA:

- Sets architecture standards for engineering teams
- Reviews system designs for internal products
- Owns technology strategy and platform selection
- Manages cross-team architectural dependencies
- Maintains architecture governance

Specialist SA:

- Deep expertise in security, data, networking, or AI/ML
- Works across multiple teams or customers
- Owns domain-specific best practices
- Often reports into a center of excellence

## How Much Coding Do SAs Need?

71.8% of SA roles require some technical hands-on ability

Most common technical skills in SA roles:

- Python scripting - 287 jobs (33.4%)
- Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation, Bicep) - 390 jobs (45.3%)
- Shell scripting (Bash) - 198 jobs (23.0%)
- SQL and data querying - 156 jobs (18.1%)
- API integration (REST, GraphQL) - 143 jobs (16.6%)

Key findings:

1. Most SAs need hands-on technical ability - 72% require some implementation skills
2. IaC is the most common technical requirement - more common than any programming language
3. Scripting dominates over application development - 2x more common for SA roles

Bottom Line: SAs need practical technical skills (IaC, scripting, API integration) but do not need to be full-stack developers. Focus on architecture patterns and cloud services first, scripting and automation second.


## What Else (Besides Cloud Knowledge) Do SAs Need?

87.3% of SA roles require skills beyond just cloud platform expertise

Skill combinations in SA roles:

- Cloud + Communication (presales, workshops, executive presentations) - 78.4%
- Cloud + Cost Optimization (TCO analysis, FinOps, reserved pricing) - 71.2%
- Cloud + Security (IAM, encryption, compliance frameworks) - 66.8%
- Cloud + Migration (assessment, planning, execution) - 54.3%
- Pure cloud design (nothing else) - 5.8%

Bottom Line: Solutions Architects are cross-functional technical leaders who specialize in cloud architecture. Only 5.8% of roles expect pure design work. Most need communication skills (presales, workshops), cost optimization, security architecture, and migration experience.
