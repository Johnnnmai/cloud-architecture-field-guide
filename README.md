## 🔄 Career Transitions

5 paths into solutions architecture, each with timeline, what transfers, and what to learn.

| From | Timeline | Your Superpower |
|------|----------|----------------|
| [Software Engineer](learning-paths/from-software-engineer.md) | 3-4 months | Application architecture, code-level understanding |
| [DevOps Engineer](learning-paths/from-devops.md) | 2-3 months | Infrastructure automation, CI/CD, monitoring |
| [Network Engineer](learning-paths/from-network-engineer.md) | 3-5 months | Network design, security, connectivity |
