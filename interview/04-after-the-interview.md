# After the Interview

What to do after completing the interview process - handling offers, rejections, and negotiations.

## Handling Rejections

Don't take rejections personally. Treat them as learning experiences. Do a retrospective: understand what went wrong. Often nothing was wrong with you personally - someone was just better, or the company ran out of budget, or priorities changed. If you lack some skill, build a proof of concept or architecture case study to improve it.

For SA roles specifically: if you failed the architecture design round, practice whiteboarding with a partner. Record yourself explaining an architecture. The communication gap is usually larger than the knowledge gap.

## Handling Offers

Don't rush into agreeing immediately. If you have interviews with other companies, finish them first. Once you get the first offer, tell other companies about it to speed up their process. Having multiple offers is the ideal situation for negotiating salary and benefits. With just one offer, negotiation is difficult.

For cloud provider SA roles (AWS, Microsoft, Google): the leveling matters as much as the base salary. Push for the right level, not just the right number.

## Salary Negotiation

If you already named a salary number in the initial recruiter call and they offer that exact number, it is very hard to negotiate upward. Two approaches to the initial salary question:

- Say the number upfront - works well when you already know the market and have experience in SA roles
- Postpone the conversation - works when transitioning from another role (DevOps, engineer), relocating, or when you don't know the SA market yet

Regardless, do research and have a number in mind. Recruiters can be pushy and may not accept "let's talk later" as an answer.

SA-specific negotiation points:

- Cloud provider SAs often have variable compensation tied to customer adoption or revenue. Understand the on-target earnings (OTE) structure.
- Certifications and specializations can justify higher compensation. If you hold AWS Professional or Specialty certifications, mention them during negotiation.
- Remote SA roles at cloud providers often have geographic pay adjustments. Ask about the compensation band for your location.
